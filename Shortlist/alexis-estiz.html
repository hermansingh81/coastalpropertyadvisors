How we’re screening these

Cap Rate = Annual Net Income ÷ Purchase Price Annual net income = (monthly rent × 12) − annual property taxes − annual common charges. Everything is converted to a yearly total first — the monthly figures on each card below are shown for readability, not used directly in the math.

This is a fast screen, not full underwriting — insurance, vacancy allowance, and management fees come later, once you’re serious about a specific unit. It's also unlevered: since you're buying in cash, this number is close to your actual return.

Figures marked confirmed are pulled directly from the current listing. Figures marked estimated are placeholders where a listing didn’t show its own numbers — worth confirming before an offer. Every property shown clears a 3% floor either way.
