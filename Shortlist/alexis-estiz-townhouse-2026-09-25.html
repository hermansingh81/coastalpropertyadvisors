How the numbers below work

Net Effective Monthly Cost = P&I + Property Tax + Insurance − Rental Income P&I assumes a 30-year fixed loan. Purchase price, down payment (as % or as a dollar amount — edit either one and the other updates), rate, and each unit's rental income are all editable on every card — change any of them and the loan amount, payment, and net cost recalculate instantly, so you can run your own scenario for each property.

Default rate: 7.375%. Freddie Mac's national average 30-year fixed rate was 7.03% for the week of September 24, 2026. Conventional loans on 2–4 unit primary-residence properties carry a modest loan-level price adjustment over a single-family primary rate at the same down payment — we've built roughly 0.375 points of that into the default shown here. Your actual quote will depend on your lender, credit, and reserves; treat this as a planning estimate and edit it once you have a real quote.

This is a financing screen, not full underwriting — closing costs, reserves, and a vacancy allowance come later, once you're serious about a specific address. Insurance is a rough estimate pending an actual broker quote.

Figures marked confirmed are cross-checked against NYC property tax records and the listing itself. Figures marked estimated are conservative, comp-based rental projections — not signed leases; each card links to the actual StreetEasy comps used. A note flags something specific worth running down before you write an offer — a steep price jump, an occupied unit, a unit count or layout question.
